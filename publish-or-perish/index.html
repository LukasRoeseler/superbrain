How this works

Enter a researcher's ORCID iD. We fetch their most-cited works from OpenAlex — each one published with a particular publisher.

The secret code is the sequence of those publishers, ordered by citation rank. Guess it row by row:

Black dot: right publisher, right rank.

Outlined dot: right publisher, wrong rank.

A joker randomly reveals the publisher for one rank.

With the timer on, you must finish your row in time, or it is submitted automatically.

In Options you can choose whether to guess 2, 3, or 4 of the researcher's top-cited works.

We automatically fetch the top-cited works (incl. publisher) via OpenAlex
